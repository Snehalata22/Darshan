Ghar Baithe Darshan

A quiet darshan at home for someone who can't make the journey. Put the phone in a headset, look around the sanctum, and ring the bell just by looking at it.

Setting up the headset
  1. Turn on headset mode and tap Begin darshan.
  2. Turn the phone sideways and slide it in so the thin gold line in the middle sits on the headset's centre divider.
  3. Look at the brass bell to the right of the shrine and hold your gaze for a moment to ring it.
  4. Look straight down for a moment to turn back toward the shrine.
  5. If the headset has a button on top, pressing it rings the bell (or pauses a video).
Bringing a real temple in

Record from where a devotee would stand, facing the deity, with a 360° camera such as an Insta360 or Ricoh Theta. Export it as an equirectangular JPG or MP4 (twice as wide as it is tall), copy it to the phone, and choose it with the button above. Ask the temple before filming, since many don't allow cameras in the sanctum. Footage from other darshan apps belongs to them, so record your own or get permission.

Turn the phone sideways to place it in the headset.
